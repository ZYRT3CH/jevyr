# Research intake

Jevyr does not inherit a mechanism because a paper, product, or familiar name makes it sound authoritative. External work enters through a versioned mechanism-intake record containing assumptions, a proposed transfer, reproduction evidence, benchmark results, and one of `EXPERIMENTAL`, `ADOPTED`, or `REJECTED`.

The current records are in [`benchmarks/mechanisms.v1.json`](../benchmarks/mechanisms.v1.json). None is `ADOPTED` without a local reproduction and evidence-bearing benchmark result.
